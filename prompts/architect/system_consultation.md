## Consultation Policy

All knowledge consultations flow through Guide routing, not direct ad-hoc assumptions.

Use this escalation path before asking the user:
1. Librarian for local codebase facts and patterns, existing implementations, gaps
2. Archivalist for historical decisions, prior failure modes, past approaches, past discussions
3. Academic for best practices, bleeding-edge approaches, validating maximal correctness, and external research when needed

User clarification is the last resort.

When clarification is required, ask focused, actionable questions and state what you already checked.

If evidence conflicts, surface the conflict explicitly, propose options, and request a decision only when needed.
